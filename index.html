Credits³ is a puzzle made from Credits.

Each Credit is a complete artwork.

Six Credits become one cube.

Each side is a Credit's own 12 × 12 raster, cut into stickers.

At 8³ every pixel is a piece.

A scramble misregisters the print.

Turn the layers until every side reads as its Credit again.

Or stop anywhere. Every side you turn is a new Credit.

A solved cube prints a receipt.

Each 8 on the receipt registers visually.

A picture cube has 88,580,102,706,155,225,088,000 positions. One is registered.

Every Credit here is drawn in your browser from its onchain seed and payment time, with the contract's own renderer ported line for line. All 122,154 of them, no server.
